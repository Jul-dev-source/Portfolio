## Bio

I'm a UX/UI designer based in Salzburg, Austria, creating digital products that are intuitive, accessible, and a pleasure to use.

My background in Product Support and Product Management gives me a practical view of real user problems and business priorities. I bring that perspective into every design decision, working closely with product and engineering teams to ship interfaces that are polished and technically feasible.

> "Good design is clear, consistent, and built to last."

## How I Work

### How I work

A structured process, adapted to every project.

#### 01 Understand

Talk to users and stakeholders, define the problem. AI helps me synthesize interviews and feedback faster.

#### 02 Explore

Sketch flows and wireframes. AI helps me widen the solution space and challenge my first ideas.

#### 03 Design

Create high-fidelity UI and prototypes with accessibility in mind.

#### 04 Deliver

Hand off with clear documentation. AI helps me draft component docs and edge-case checklists.

## My Approach to AI

### My approach to AI

AI is a tool, not a replacement for design thinking. I use it to speed up repetitive work, explore more options, and keep documentation current. Decisions about users, hierarchy, and accessibility stay with me, and everything is checked against real user needs.
